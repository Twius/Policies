- If your device is lost, damaged, reset, or has the app uninstalled, your Sifdroid data is gone for good. We cannot get it back for you, because we never had it.

You are responsible for what you enter and for keeping your device secure. The optional app lock is an extra layer, not a replacement for a screen lock on the phone itself.
